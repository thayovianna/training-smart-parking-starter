# Definition of Ready

A backlog item can be considered ready for refinement or planning when the team has enough information to understand the problem, discuss the solution space, and make an informed delivery decision.

This Definition of Ready is a working agreement, not a rigid gate.

---

## Ready Criteria

A backlog item should meet the following criteria:

- The relevant user or stakeholder is identified.
- The problem or need is clear.
- The expected user or business value is understood.
- The item is connected to a relevant product or business goal.
- Relevant evidence is referenced when available.
- Important assumptions are explicitly identified.
- Dependencies are known or explicitly marked as unknown.
- Major risks have been considered.
- Acceptance criteria are understandable and testable when appropriate.
- Relevant unanswered questions are visible.
- The item is small enough to be discussed, refined, and estimated by the team.

---

## Important Guidance

A backlog item does not need to have every implementation detail defined before refinement.

The purpose of this agreement is to make uncertainty visible, not to hide it.

If important information is missing, the item should clearly state:

- what is unknown;
- why it matters;
- what needs to be investigated;
- who may help answer it.

---

## AI Usage Guidance

AI may help:

- identify missing information;
- detect assumptions;
- compare an item with this Definition of Ready;
- suggest questions for stakeholders;
- identify possible risks or dependencies.

AI should not independently declare a product decision correct or final.

Readiness remains a team decision.
