# Smart Parking — Stakeholders

This document captures known stakeholder needs and tensions for the Smart Parking product.

## Employee

### Need
Know before leaving home whether parking is likely to be available.

### Tension
Wants predictability with as little friction as possible.

---

## Facilities

### Need
Manage parking capacity and reduce misuse or idle reserved spaces.

### Tension
Does not want long reservations that reduce effective capacity.

---

## Security

### Need
Know which vehicles are expected when access-control policies require it.

### Tension
Needs enough information for control without unnecessary data collection.

---

## Management

### Need
Improve utilization of the existing parking capacity.

### Tension
Does not want a booking process that becomes too complex for employees.

---

## Working Note

Stakeholder requests are inputs, not automatically product requirements.
When needs conflict, make the conflict visible and prepare trade-offs for a Product Visionary decision.
