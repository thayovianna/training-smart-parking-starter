# Smart Parking — Business Goals

## Primary Objective

Reduce the time and uncertainty associated with finding parking at the office.

---

# Goal 1 — Reduce Time Spent Searching for Parking

## Desired Outcome

Employees should spend less time driving around the parking area looking for a space.

## Indicator

Average reported time between entering the parking area and parking the vehicle.

## Baseline

Still needs to be measured.

## Initial Target

30% reduction compared with the baseline.

## Note

The target should be reviewed once we have enough real data.

---

# Goal 2 — Increase Predictability for Employees

## Desired Outcome

Before leaving home, employees should be able to make a more informed decision about whether to use the office parking area.

## Possible Indicators

- percentage of users who are able to check availability;
- satisfaction related to predictability;
- reduction in reports of arriving when no parking space is available.

## Target

Not yet defined.

We first need to establish a baseline.

---

# Goal 3 — Improve Use of Existing Parking Spaces

## Desired Outcome

Reduce periods when parking spaces appear occupied or reserved but are not actually being used.

## Possible Indicators

- utilization rate;
- percentage of unused reservations;
- average duration of parking spaces being blocked without actual use.

## Target

Not yet defined.

---

# Goal 4 — Keep the Experience Simple

## Desired Outcome

Smart Parking should not turn a simple task into a bureaucratic process.

## Possible Indicators

- time required to check parking availability;
- number of steps required;
- process abandonment rate;
- qualitative user feedback.

---

# Guardrails

Business improvements must not be achieved at the expense of:

- security;
- privacy;
- accessibility;
- simplicity of the experience.

For example:

We should not collect additional personal data simply because it might be useful in the future.

---

# Open Questions

We still need to discover:

- What is the current average time spent searching for parking?
- How many people avoid going to the office because of parking?
- What is the actual parking capacity?
- What is the average occupancy rate?
- Are there significant differences by day of the week?
- How many spaces remain unavailable even though they are not being used?
