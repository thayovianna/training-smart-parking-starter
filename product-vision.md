# Smart Parking — Product Vision

## Vision

We want to make the office parking experience more predictable, simple, and efficient.

Today, employees may arrive at the office without knowing whether they will find an available parking space.

This leads to:

- wasted time;
- frustration;
- unnecessary driving around the parking area;
- difficulty planning the trip to the office;
- inefficient use of the available parking spaces.

Smart Parking should help employees make better decisions before and during their arrival at the office.

---

## Problem We Want to Solve

Employees do not have enough visibility into parking availability before arriving at the office.

As a result, some people:

- arrive earlier just to increase their chances of finding a space;
- waste time searching for parking;
- avoid going to the office on certain days;
- rely on informal information from colleagues.

---

## Who We Are Building For

### Employees

People who go to the office and need to park.

Primary need:

> Know whether there is a reasonable chance of finding parking before starting the trip.

### Facilities

The team responsible for managing parking spaces and the parking area.

Primary need:

> Understand how the existing parking capacity is being used.

### Security

The team responsible for controlling access to the parking area.

Primary need:

> Ensure that access continues to comply with security rules.

---

## Expected Outcome

We want employees to be able to plan their trip to the office with greater confidence and spend less time searching for parking.

The product should also help the organization make better use of the existing parking capacity.

---

## Product Principles

### Solve the Problem Before Adding Features

We should not add features without understanding which user or business problem they solve.

### Simplicity

Using Smart Parking should require as few steps as possible.

### Transparency

The system should clearly distinguish whether information represents:

- current availability;
- a prediction;
- a reservation;
- or an estimate.

### Privacy

We should request only the data that is actually necessary to provide the service.

### Fast Feedback

We prefer to deliver smaller increments, observe user behavior, and learn before expanding the solution.

---

## What We Still Do Not Know

We still need to understand:

- how far in advance users need this information;
- whether availability information is enough or reservation is necessary;
- how long a reservation should remain valid;
- how to handle people who reserve and do not show up;
- which vehicle data is actually necessary;
- how visitors should be handled.

These points should be investigated before being treated as definitive requirements.

---

## Out of Scope for the Initial Phase

At this point, we do not have enough evidence to include:

- parking payments;
- public parking;
- multi-day parking reservations;
- visual parking-space selection on a map;
- integration with vehicle navigation.

These ideas may be revisited if new needs or evidence justify them.
